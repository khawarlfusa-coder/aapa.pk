Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: September 2026

Welcome to Aapa.PK ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your information is gathered, utilized, and protected when you visit our health blog or order wellness products through our nationwide Cash on Delivery (COD) service.

3. We comply with Google AdSense privacy terms regarding transparent third-party cookie disclosures.

The personal information you provide during checkout is used strictly for:
